1Heath: The Visit Writes the History

A medical scribe and shared record for India's small clinics. The doctor talks; 1Heath writes a checked note and prescription in the doctor's language; the doctor slides to sign; the record follows the patient.

FounderPawan Kalyan
StatusWorking demo, not yet in clinics
Updated6 November 2026
Key Points
  • Indian doctors usually treat patients without their history. Our estimate: about 6,400 hospital deaths and 1.6 lakh severe harms a year in India come from medication errors caused by an incomplete history (method and ranges).
  • 1Heath listens to the consultation, drafts the record with an India-hosted language model, and checks every line with a rules engine before the doctor signs.
  • On simulated tests, no invented allergy, refused medicine or wrong dose reached a note without being flagged.
  • It is not ready for real patients yet; the sections below say exactly what is missing.

Terms Used on These Pages

ABHA
Ayushman Bharat Health Account: India's 14-digit health ID for each person.
ABDM
Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission: the government's system for health IDs, registries and consent-based record sharing.
AYUSH
Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Sowa-Rigpa and Homoeopathy.
OPD
Outpatient department: patients seen and sent home the same day.
Scribe
Software (or a person) that writes the clinical note from the conversation.
Verifier
1Heath's rules engine that checks each drafted line against the words spoken and the drug catalogue.
Anupana
In Ayurveda, the vehicle a medicine is taken with, such as warm water, honey or milk.
Potency
In homeopathy, the dilution of a remedy, such as 30C, 200 or 1M.
Look-alike names
Brand names that sound or look similar but are different drugs, a known cause of prescribing errors.
HLD and LLD
High-level design (the components) and low-level design (the data model and rules inside them).
FHIR
A global standard format for exchanging health records, used by ABDM.
DPDP Act
India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
